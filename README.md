This is a dungeon-crawler, text-based game. The player moves around with N, S, E, or W to explore the dungeon. On each floor, you will need to complete some goal to open the door deeper and then return to the starting room via walking or ‘TP’ ing. Once the goal is fulfilled, you can ‘Descend’ (just ‘D’ also works) to go to the next floor. Currently, you will need to enter the command twice, once to open the door and once to enter it, before you go to the next floor. As you explore the dungeon, you will find different kinds of rooms, including rooms with enemies to fight. The combat system is kinda broken and very cheeseable, but it kind of balances out with other parts of the game being harder than intended.

I have ideas on how to improve my code. The current version has significant technical debt, as I only wanted a working prototype and took sub-optimal options. As said, I intend to fix it and make it more developer-friendly, but that will likely take a full rewrite, something I probably won't do for a while.
